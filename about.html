What this site is

This is the individual portfolio site for the Individual Website deliverable in EST 240. The site hosts three artifacts:

  1. Hungry Gaze, the research essay analyzing the visual meaning-making practices of the PAIK JONG WON YouTube channel through Chandler's film grammar, Barthes's myth theory, and Mulvey's gaze.
  2. Channel Analysis, two embedded videos from the Baegopa series with shot-by-shot visual grammar and semiotic analysis.
  3. The Logo, the AI-generated personal mark in three sizes plus the AI prompts used to construct it, and the 15-second AI cinematic video.

Analytical Framework

Framework 1

Film Grammar

Shot size, camera angle, editing, sound, and mise-en-scène as described by Chandler in "The Grammar of Television and Film."

Framework 2

Semiotics

Denotation, connotation, and myth as described by Barthes and applied to visual media by Fiske. Sign theory via Saussure and Peirce.

Framework 3

The Look

The gaze, identification, scopophilia, and desire as developed by Mulvey and Pennings in the Ways of Looking module.

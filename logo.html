Module 8 · Visual Identity

The Logo

The spoon that looks back.

A spoon. Look at the bowl — there is an eye staring back. One shape carries both readings simultaneously: the spoon means hunger, the eye means gaze. You cannot separate them. That is the point.

Why this mark works

Originality A spoon whose bowl contains an eye — this specific combination does not exist elsewhere. The mark is impossible to confuse with any other brand because it requires the exact overlap of the two concepts this site is built on.
Single shape This is not two elements placed next to each other. The spoon bowl is the eye. One silhouette, two simultaneous readings. The mark cannot be simplified further without destroying one of the two meanings.
Scale At 28px the oval spoon bowl and pupil dot are still legible. The handle extends to give directional movement. At 300px the duality is fully visible.
Denotation An oval spoon bowl with a filled circle at its center, and a curved handle extending to the lower right.
Connotation The spoon connotes eating, food, mukbang, the act of bringing food to the mouth. The filled circle inside reads as a pupil — an eye staring from within the spoon. Together: the site's name. A hungry gaze. The spoon looks at you as you look at food.
Myth (Barthes) The mark naturalizes what the essay argues: that watching food and eating food are the same desire. The spoon-eye collapses the act of looking and the act of consuming into a single shape — which is precisely what the Baegopa series does to its viewers.

The AI prompts

Tool: Anthropic Claude (claude-sonnet-4-6), concept development and direct SVG generation.

Prompt 1 · Core constraint

I need a logo for "Hungry Gaze." All previous attempts had the two concepts side by side — chopsticks next to an eye, arc next to text. I need a single shape that IS both concepts simultaneously, like the FedEx arrow. The shape should be impossible to see as only one thing. "Hungry" and "gaze" must be the same object.

Prompt 2 · Direction chosen

I want the spoon-eye concept. A spoon (food, hunger, mukbang) where the oval bowl of the spoon contains a pupil dot — making the bowl of the spoon simultaneously the iris of an eye. One shape reads as both. The handle grounds it as a spoon so the first reading is always "spoon," then the pupil reveals the eye. Accent color #c8651a. Minimal strokes.

Prompt 3 · Sizing and wordmark

Three variants: (1) Large — spoon-eye in orange + rule + "youngjae" italic serif + "HUNGRY · GAZE" tagline in mono. (2) Medium B&W — same mark in black, wordmark only. (3) Small icon — white spoon-eye mark on solid orange circle badge, no text. The icon must read at 28px in a navigation bar.

Prompt 4 · Semiotic framing

Describe the mark using the semiotic vocabulary of the course. Explain the denotation, connotation, and how the mark encodes the Barthesian myth that the essay identifies in the Baegopa series — that looking at food and consuming food are the same act of desire.
